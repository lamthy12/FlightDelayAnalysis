# FlightDelayAnalysis

Course Team Project | BANA 270: Intro to Business Analytics and AI
March - June 2025
Tools I used: Altair AI Studio, Powerpoint

1. A Brief Overview of the Project
My team's Flight Project provides a clear vision of delay classification by using logistic regression to propose ways for companies and customers with model prediction. Not only applying predictive analytics to airline operations, but also addresses the challenges and recommend possible outcomes for operation improvement in airline industry.

2. My Contribution
I developed business process recommendations and management plan for applying the team's predictive model to airline operations
- Suggested real-time integrating model predictions into scheduling dashboard (e.g API, DSS), decision support systems will help teams handle large data and access results without interpreting complex data.
- Recommend collaboration between operations, revenue management and customer service teams to optimize departure scheduling decisions and passenger updates.
- Proposed performance metrics and feedback mechanisms to help solving departmental concerns about pricing, technical integration, implementation costs and change current workflows.
- Improved the business value and on-time performance (OTP) to secure flight patterns.

3. Project Description
My recommendations support airline companies on how to use the model in daily operations:
- Forecast and report delay risks: Use APIs to connect model results to a dashboard or DSS database so the team can view predictive output in one place.
- Improve teamwork and increase flexibility.
- Track potential business value: Provide customer service where clients are less likely to face problems (e.g automatic notifications in phone, or airline app: "Your Delay Risk Score" for upcoming flights). Also, monitor on-time performance, and operating costs to ensure whether the tool helps the airline.
